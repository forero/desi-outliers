\section{Loa Outlier Summary}

Table~\ref{tab:loa_summary} summarises the total number of tiles and
UMAP-identified outlier spectra in the Loa spectroscopic production,
restricted to main-survey tiles.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Total tiles and outliers in the Loa main survey by program.}
\label{tab:loa_summary}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\hline
Program & Tiles & Outliers \\
\hline
Dark   & 6{,}671  & 191{,}411 \\
Bright & 5{,}165  & 463{,}543 \\
Backup & 1{,}310  & 349{,}927 \\
\hline
Total  & 13{,}146 & 1{,}004{,}881 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The total number of outliers across all main-survey programs exceeds one million,
with a global rate of $\sim$76.5 outliers per tile.
The bright program has roughly 2.4 times more outliers than the dark program
despite covering fewer tiles, reflecting the higher outlier fraction
per tile in bright observations ($\sim$89.7 outliers/tile)
compared to dark observations ($\sim$28.7 outliers/tile).
The backup program, despite the smallest tile count, contributes
349{,}927 outliers ($\sim$267 outliers/tile), the highest rate of the three programs.
