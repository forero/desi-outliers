\section{Loa Outlier Summary}

Table~\ref{tab:loa_survey} shows the total number of tiles and
UMAP-identified outlier spectra in the Loa spectroscopic production,
broken down by survey type.
The full catalog contains 1{,}095{,}816 outliers across 14{,}160 tiles.
The main survey accounts for 1{,}004{,}881 outliers (91.7\%) distributed
over 13{,}146 tiles, with the remainder coming from commissioning (CMX),
special, and survey-validation (SV1, SV2, SV3) observations.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Total tiles and outliers in the Loa production by survey.}
\label{tab:loa_survey}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\hline
Survey  & Tiles  & Outliers \\
\hline
CMX     &      1 &         49 \\
Main    & 13{,}146 & 1{,}004{,}881 \\
Special &    303 &     21{,}301 \\
SV1     &    185 &     27{,}401 \\
SV2     &     37 &      2{,}839 \\
SV3     &    488 &     39{,}345 \\
\hline
Total   & 14{,}160 & 1{,}095{,}816 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Table~\ref{tab:loa_summary} further breaks down the main-survey outliers
by observing program.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Total tiles, outliers, and outlier rate in the Loa main survey by program.}
\label{tab:loa_summary}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\hline
Program & Tiles & Outliers & Outliers/tile \\
\hline
Dark   & 6{,}671  & 191{,}411 &  28.69 \\
Bright & 5{,}165  & 463{,}543 &  89.75 \\
Backup & 1{,}310  & 349{,}927 & 267.12 \\
\hline
Total  & 13{,}146 & 1{,}004{,}881 & 76.44 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The total number of main-survey outliers exceeds one million,
with a global rate of 76.44 outliers per tile.
The bright program has roughly 2.4 times more outliers than the dark program
despite covering fewer tiles, reflecting the higher outlier rate
per tile in bright observations (89.75 outliers/tile)
compared to dark observations (28.69 outliers/tile).
The backup program, despite the smallest tile count, contributes
349{,}927 outliers at 267.12 outliers/tile, the highest rate of the three programs.
